What the work is for

Be found. Be chosen.

Here is an ordinary example. Someone wants a coffee and opens Maps. A café nearby comes up. Before they go, they look at the menu, check the hours and tap for directions.

The café was found, because it showed up where that person was already looking. Then it was chosen, because the menu, the hours and the directions were there before anyone had to ask.

Be found
Show up where your customer is already looking: Google Search, Maps, social media, ads and, more and more, AI answers.
Be chosen
Once they've found you, give them enough to feel sure: clear information, real work, genuine reviews, honest prices and one simple next step, to call, enquire, book or buy.

Ads and search help people discover a business. The website, the work and the reviews help them decide.
